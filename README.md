## 💡 How Procedura Works

Procedura uses something called "agentic 3D modeling." Here's what that means in plain language:

1. **You Describe**: You type your idea in everyday words.
2. **Procedura Understands**: A smart AI assistant reads your description and figures out what you want.
3. **Procedura Builds**: It creates a parametric program—think of it as a recipe with adjustable ingredients.
4. **You Control**: You can change any aspect of your model—size, shape, material, movement—just by adjusting simple sliders or typing new values.

This "shape-as-code" approach means your 3D model isn't just a fixed image. It's a living, breathing design that you can modify anytime. Want to make your chair taller? Change one number. Want to make your robot arm bend differently? Move one slider. It's that flexible.
